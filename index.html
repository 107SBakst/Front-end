Live gdtv2-eosin.vercel.app

GDT · Global Data Technology

Official statistics get revised after first publication. GDT saves each new version it finds, so you can see what was known on any past day, not just the latest figure. It pulls from sources such as the US BEA, UK ONS, Eurostat and the Bank of Japan, and serves them through a web explorer and an API.

  • Every revision kept. Each release is saved as a new version, and nothing is overwritten.
  • Release-aware fetching. Series are fetched on a daily schedule, with extra checks around known release times.
  • Public docs, keyed data. Modelled on FRED's API; the docs are public, and reading data needs a read key.
